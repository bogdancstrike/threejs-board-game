# Configuration, card path and world expansion

Goal: a highly configurable game with a real configuration page, a card on every Adventure tile, cleaner card text, and more worlds. Deploy with `docker compose up -d --build` after each milestone.

1. **Configuration page** (`src/ui/config-page.ts`, `src/settings/preferences.ts`, `src/world/graphics.ts`). Sections: Player profile (name, favourite hero with live 3D preview, aura, default difficulty/party/rules/road adventures), Display & performance (Low/Medium/High/Ultra preset, render scale, dynamic resolution, FPS cap, shadow resolution, FXAA, shadows, AO, FPS meter), Lighting & atmosphere (8 moods incl. dynamic day/night, storm lightning and aurora; sun direction; day length; exposure; bloom; haze; weather; wind), Colour & water (saturation, contrast, vignette, water/foam/spray), Gameplay (movement speed, run, camera follow, tooltips, legend, pass-the-device prompts, offline party), Camera (FOV, orbit/zoom speed, invert, title orbit), Audio, Accessibility (UI scale, reduced motion). All persisted and validated. **Implemented.**
2. **Card on every tile.** Adventure rooms: named roadside stops keep their encounter once per player; every other tile from 1 to 91 applies its tile reward, then draws a party card. Floating instanced card markers show on each card tile. **Implemented; backend tests cover all 91 tiles.**
3. **Card text.** Remove disclaimer-style asides from prompts and UI notes; decks grow to 300 unique cards per level (25 activities × 12 themes). **Implemented; a test bans the asides and enforces ≥250 unique cards per level.**
4. **Worlds 30 → 36.** Shattered Glacier, Redstone Canyon, Lanternmire, Starforge Ring (with skyway), Saltglass Shoals, Ember Foundry: shapes, geography, landmarks, catalogue, previews. World gallery gains family filters, sorting and "Surprise me". **Implemented; previews pending render.**
5. Release: unit/backend/e2e suites, preview refresh, README, commit and push.
