# Art direction & environments
## Palette
Twilight background #101322, moon blue #A7B9FF, glowing purple #A855F7, magical cyan #38D9FF, amber fire #FFAD55, warm windows #FFD18B, forest #3E806F, cherry blossom #F472B6.

## Style rules
Hand-painted, slightly simplified 3D fantasy miniature; rounded silhouette and beveled geometry; avoid mixing hyperreal scans with low-poly cartoon assets. No oversized environment elements that hide the tile path. Visual storytelling is local to each biome; readable route has priority over clutter.

## Asset taxonomy
- Rock/cliffs 6–10 reusable forms, terrain meshes and ramps.
- Buildings 5–8: blacksmith, cottage, mill, castle/towers, market, shrine, ruins.
- Vegetation 10–15: pines, flowering trees, shrubs, grasses, mushrooms.
- Traversal 3–5 bridge/stairs variations.
- Magic 5–8 crystal, rune, portal and shrine props.
- Dressing 10–15: lanterns, barrels, signs, crates, fences, furniture.

## Rough scale
Player pawn silhouette approximately one tile tall from base to head (adjust to readability), buildings roughly 2.5–6 pawn heights, castle towers 6–9 pawn heights *but placed behind playable route*. These are proportion targets, not fixed engineering units.

## Scene mood
Moonlit twilight with warm light islands in village and castle. Distant floating landmasses fade into fog; slight breeze animates trees, fabric and flags; rivers connect all terraces. Emissive crystals and lanterns are focal accents rather than every surface glowing.

## Asset selection standards
Use game-ready glTF/GLB assets where possible, consistent hand-painted materials, documented model scale/origin, clean transforms, clear commercial license and attribution. Do not use original Țapul Țicu graphics or distinctive characters without rights. See `ASSET_PIPELINE.md`.
