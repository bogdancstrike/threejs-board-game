# Țapul Țicu — historical investigation and evidence ledger
**Evidence standard:** Confirmed = source explicitly states it; Plausible = supported by indirect accounts; Unknown = no reliable verification. Do not present design adaptations as original features.

## Confirmed
- Romanian-language educational PC game titles associated with **Aidem Media / Erc Press** are archived as four distinct releases: **Țapul inventator**, **Țapul Țicu este la grădiniță**, **Țapul Țicu este la școală**, **Țapul Țicu merge la școală**. Archive collection lists them by these names [S1].
- `Țapul Țicu merge la școală` was catalogued by Erc Press in **2003**, ISBN **973-7977-29-7**, within *Să ne jucăm pe calculator* [S2].
- `Țapul inventator` marketing/catalog copy explicitly describes **ten new games** revolving around discovering a recipe for top grades; subjects include arithmetic/letters, climate, weight/length/capacity, clocks, colors/shapes, and elementary mechanics [S3].
- The `Țapul Țicu este la grădiniță` title was covered in a 2008 magazine post; that post calls it a **book**, so it does not independently verify its precise in-game content [S4].
- Internet Archive hosts public listings for ISO images; this demonstrates preservation efforts, **not that the collection's declared CC0 status transfers rights in every original game's content** [S1].
- Romanian players discuss recovering and running CD-era Erc Press games in virtual machines/compatibility modes; these are anecdotal community accounts [S5].

## Unverified — must not fabricate
- A specific Țapul Țicu board with dice, snakes, 60–93 numbered squares, or multiplayer races.
- Any canonical board geometry, map regions, color palette, UI placement, tile probabilities, scoring formula, or pawn mechanics.
- Exact animation sequences, screenshots and 3D architecture of the original titles.
- A common single gameplay engine across all releases.
- That internet claims of app-store versions, exact module counts, friends' names, or free commercial downloads on SEO-generated sites are authentic. Conflicting claims exist; treat these sites as low confidence.

## Likely gameplay identity, stated carefully
The commercial descriptions support the framing **educational activities/minigames tied together by an animal protagonist**, not sufficient evidence for a board-game ruleset. It is reasonable to borrow the *design values*—approachable interactions, playful challenges, recognizable thematic regions, progression, audiovisual feedback—but NOT to claim these exact rules belonged to Țapul Țicu.

## Visual appearance / maps
There are no authenticated, inspected in-game screenshots in this investigation. Accordingly, **no exact historical map/scene appearance is asserted**. ## How to conduct a faithful forensic study later
1. Independently establish permission to access and use old game media; preserve originals read-only.
2. Obtain legitimately held discs or archive ISOs and hashes; run in isolated offline Windows VM, no network access; take snapshots.
3. Record title/version, start screen, every menu, actual map screenshot, transitions, mini-game list, scoring/rules, input behavior, audio and duration.
4. Capture screenshots and an annotated video with per-screen timestamps; document observed vs inferred mechanics.
5. Build a feature provenance matrix: original observed / anecdotal / adaptation only.
6. Do not redistribute original binaries, textures, music, voice or logos in new product without license.
